How to play Ember Duel

Each turn you gain mana and draw a card. Click a card (or press 1–7, then Enter) to play it. Attack damages the foe's shield first, then their life. Shield cards protect you. Healing restores life. Draw cards refill your hand. Mana cards give extra mana this turn.

Ember Chain: playing two cards of the same suit in one turn gives the second card +2 attack. Watch Cinder's last suit — chain yours, and break theirs.

Suits: 🔥 Flame hits hard · 🍃 Leaf shields and heals · 💧 Tide draws and feeds mana · ⚡ Spark bursts with extra tricks.

First fighter to 0 life loses. Good luck, Keeper.
